## Tiger Cubs Class
Our Tiger Cubs class is designed for children ages 4–5. This playful and structured class introduces young students to Taekwondo while helping them develop important life skills in a fun, safe, and positive environment.
****
### Class Goals

**Focus:**
Students learn to listen, follow simple instructions, and stay engaged through fun, age-appropriate activities.

**Confidence:**
By learning new skills and reaching small goals, children build confidence and pride in their accomplishments.

**Respect:**
Tiger Cubs are taught to show respect to their instructors, classmates, and parents, both in and out of the studio.

**Basic Movement & Martial Arts Skills:**
Students practice basic Taekwondo movements, balance, and coordination, helping improve motor skills and prepare them for future classes.
****

### Class Schedule
Our Tiger Cubs class traditionally runs twice a week. Please refer to our schedule for the exact days of the week and times.
