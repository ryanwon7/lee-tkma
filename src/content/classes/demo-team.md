## Demo Team Class
Our Demo Team class is for dedicated students who want to take their Taekwondo skills to the next level. This class focuses on advanced techniques, exciting combinations, and working together as a team to create polished performances.
****
### Class Goals

**Advanced Skills & Combinations:**
Students train more advanced techniques, forms, and combinations that go beyond regular class material, helping sharpen speed, power, and control.

**Teamwork:**
Demo Team members learn how to work together, support one another, and move as a group. Teamwork is a key part of every practice and performance.

**Choreography & Performance:**
Students learn choreographed routines that combine martial arts skills with timing, rhythm, and presentation to create strong, confident performances.

**Competition & Demonstrations:**
The Demo Team competes in local competitions twice a year and performs at our biannual belt ceremonies, giving students opportunities to showcase their skills in front of an audience.
****
